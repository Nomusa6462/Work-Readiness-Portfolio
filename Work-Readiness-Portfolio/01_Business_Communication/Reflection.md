# Reflection: Business Communication

**S – Situation:**  
During the Business Communication training, I was introduced to professional workplace communication, including email etiquette, report writing, and presentation skills. One of our key exercises involved composing a formal business email to a potential employer.

**T – Task:**  
The task required me to write a professional email that demonstrated clarity, respect, and proper structure. It needed to reflect an understanding of appropriate tone and format for a workplace setting.

**A – Action:**  
I researched professional email examples and followed the structure taught in class — including a clear subject line, a polite greeting, and a concise message. I then reviewed my draft to remove any informal language and ensured my message was free of grammatical errors.

**R – Result:**  
The feedback I received highlighted my strong structure and tone. Through this activity, I learned how professional communication builds credibility and improves workplace relationships. I now apply these skills when writing formal correspondence.
