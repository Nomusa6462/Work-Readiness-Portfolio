# Reflection: Workplace Etiquette

**S – Situation:**  
The Workplace Etiquette module focused on appropriate behavior, teamwork, and professionalism in the workplace. We discussed topics such as punctuality, dress code, respect, and communication with colleagues and supervisors.

**T – Task:**  
I was required to identify and reflect on the key principles of workplace etiquette that contribute to a positive and respectful work environment.

**A – Action:**  
I engaged in class discussions, reviewed real-world examples, and reflected on my past experiences in professional or academic settings. I took note of behaviors that promote teamwork and harmony in diverse workplaces.

**R – Result:**  
I learned that professionalism goes beyond appearance — it’s about attitude, respect, and reliability. Understanding workplace etiquette has prepared me to adapt easily to organizational cultures and to maintain a positive, cooperative work ethic.
